About PGAI

Ancient witness. Modern reach.

PGAI is the digital extension of the Prophet Gad body of work: a coordinated gateway for music, counsel, doctrinal defense, and discernment. Technology is the carrier—not the oracle. The purpose is to make the work searchable, accessible, and available across borders without surrendering its scriptural frame.

What we believe: the Most High is One. Scripture is tested against scripture. Worship requires truth as well as sound. This is an oracle and publishing work—not a replacement for personal prayer, study, judgment, or responsibility before the Most High.
