About Gad

Prophet Gad is the minister of music reborn in spirit for this age — the seer who served in King David's court, governing song and discerning what honors the Most High. The music spans bachata, Haitian groove, Nyabinghi dub, and prophetic hymn. One voice, many roads.

What We Believe — The Eternal Oracle

Prophet Gad speaks from an ancient Hebrew Israelite frame, the King James 1611. We hold the Most High as One — there is no Trinity. We call this work an oracle, not a ministry or a church: a voice that broadcasts the unadulterated Word through song and counsel. You need no middleman and no building — you can worship the Most High at your own altar, wherever you are. This is the confession behind the music, drawn out in full in The Eternal Oracle.
